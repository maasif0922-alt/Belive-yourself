Transparency is core to our mission.

IMPORTANT: We only recommend products we genuinely believe in. Our affiliate relationships do not compromise our editorial independence.

In compliance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines, we want to be fully transparent about our relationships with the companies and products we recommend. Believe Yourself is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. 3. What This Means for You

Clicking on an affiliate link does NOT increase the price you pay for a product. The commission we earn is paid directly by the retailer (Amazon or AliExpress) at no additional cost to you. We do not negotiate special deals or higher prices for our readers.

4. Our Review Integrity Pledge

Our commitment is to you, our reader. Our reviews and recommendations are based on our own independent research, testing, and analysis. Affiliate commissions NEVER influence our ratings, reviews, or the products we choose to feature. We will always give our honest opinions, highlighting both the pros and cons of a product, regardless of any potential commission.

5. How to Identify Affiliate Links

We strive to make our affiliate links clear and recognizable. You will typically see affiliate links associated with buttons such as "Check Price on Amazon" or "View Deal on AliExpress." We also provide an affiliate disclosure statement at the top of every page containing affiliate links.
