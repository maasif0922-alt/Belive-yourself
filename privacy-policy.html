Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Believe Yourself ("we," "us," or "our"). We operate the website located at believeyourself.deals (the "Site"), which functions as an affiliate marketing platform helping consumers discover quality products on Amazon and AliExpress.

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

As the data controller for this website, Believe Yourself takes its obligations seriously under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union/EEA, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California residents.

🛡️ Our Commitment: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your privacy is fundamental to how we operate.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

Personal Information

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Subscribe to our newsletter — We collect your name (optional) and email address.
  • Submit our contact form — We collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Leave a comment or review — We collect your name, email address, and submitted content.

Usage Data

When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior, including:

  • IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URLs
  • Date and time of your visit

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. See Section 4 for full details on our cookie usage.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Newsletter delivery: To send you weekly deals, product reviews, and buying guides that you have subscribed to receive.
  • Responding to inquiries: To respond to your contact form submissions and provide customer support.
  • Site improvement: To analyze usage patterns, identify technical issues, and improve the quality and relevance of our content.
  • Analytics: To understand our audience demographics and content performance.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, security risks, and technical issues.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our Site uses the following types of cookies:

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized data about how visitors use our Site. This includes page views, session duration, traffic sources, and geographic location at a country/region level. Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under its own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Affiliate Tracking Cookies

When you click on affiliate links to Amazon or AliExpress, those platforms may set their own tracking cookies on your browser. These cookies allow them to attribute any subsequent purchases to our affiliate account. We do not control these third-party cookies.

How to Opt Out of Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our Site. For more information about managing cookies, visit allaboutcookies.org.

📊 Google Analytics Disclosure: This site uses Google Analytics. Data collected is anonymous and aggregated. We have enabled IP anonymization to further protect user privacy. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Affiliate Partners

We participate in the Amazon Associates Program and the AliExpress Affiliate Program. When you click a link to their platforms, those companies may collect data about your visit and purchase in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not transmit your personal data to these affiliate partners directly.

Analytics Service Providers

We use Google Analytics (a service of Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA) to help us understand site usage. Data shared with Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Email Service Providers

We may use a third-party email service provider (such as Mailchimp or ConvertKit) to send our newsletter. Your email address is shared with this provider solely for the purpose of sending you emails you have subscribed to.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities.

6. Your Rights

GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent to process your data.

🇪🇺 GDPR Notice: To exercise any of your GDPR rights, please contact us at privacy@believeyourself.deals. We will respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

CCPA Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the CCPA:

  • Right to Know: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell personal information, so this right is inherently satisfied.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

🇺🇸 CCPA Notice: California residents may submit requests to exercise their rights by emailing privacy@believeyourself.deals. We will not sell your personal information.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for the following periods:

  • Newsletter subscribers: Your email address is retained until you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any newsletter email.
  • Contact form submissions: Messages and associated contact data are retained for up to 90 days after your inquiry is resolved, then securely deleted.
  • Analytics data: Aggregated, anonymized usage data may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked back to individual users.
  • Legal obligations: We may retain certain data longer if required to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 16 years. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@believeyourself.deals and we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Notify newsletter subscribers by email of significant changes.
  • Where required by law, obtain your consent before implementing changes.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@believeyourself.deals
  • Website: Contact Form

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
